📖 Official Ludo Rules

1. Releasing Tokens (Roll a 6)

A pawn can only leave the base yard onto the starting cell when you roll a 6.

2. Bonus Turns

You earn an immediate extra roll whenever you:

  • Roll a 6 (up to 2 times in a row).
  • Capture an opponent's token.
  • Successfully guide a token into the HOME goal.

3. Three Consecutive 6s Penalty

Rolling three 6s in a row forfeits the third roll and your turn automatically passes to the next player.

4. Safe Zones (Stars & Starts)

Tokens standing on Star cells (★) or on any player's Starting square are completely immune from capture!

5. Capturing Opponents

Landing on an opponent's token on a normal (non-safe) square knocks it out, sending it back to their yard and granting you a bonus turn!

6. Reaching Home & Winning

Tokens must complete the 52-step circuit and enter their color's home stretch. An exact roll is required to reach the center Home triangle (step 56). The first player to bring all 4 tokens Home wins 1st Place!
